# Prestofiles

**Free online tools for images, PDFs and documents.**

🌐 **Website:** [https://prestofiles.com](https://prestofiles.com)

Resize and compress images, compress, merge and split PDFs, and more. All tools are free, work on mobile and desktop, and need no signup.

## Why Prestofiles?

- **Fast:** most tools run in your browser, so results are instant.
- **Private:** image and PDF tools never upload your files. They stay in your browser.
- **Free on any device:** no signup and no watermark, on phone or desktop.
